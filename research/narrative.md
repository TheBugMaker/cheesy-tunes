# Narrative

Michael Provelone was a simple soul and never wanted to do anything else than become a Dairy Farmer. But life wasn't kind to him. First he was drafted into the Second Feta War, a conflict so briny that nobody came home unsalted.. When he came back, his family wanted him to join the Family Business - smuggling camembert with mushrooms, making nacho cheese sauce in dark basements during the probation, and collecting protection money with the cheeseknife ready to cut a slice. Michael wanted nothing to do with it, but than a rival family tried to murder his father with poisoned cheese. It left Michael no chance but to get involved. He met the head of the rival family, and through a big-cheese move, he managed to throw Mac'n'Cheese at the rival - a Mac'n'Cheese instant mix, a bad off-brand instant mix. No insult was lower than covering a man in partially powdered cheese from an unlicensed packet. The rival was humiliated in a way that he could never recover and the Family Business was safe, at least for a few more years.. But Michael had to flee.

Michael returned to his family’s homeland to hide from the consequences of the actions he never wanted to take, but could never escape. He started to help his distant uncle with the more legit family business the uncle started: making cheese. At first, the work was honest. Milk, salt, patience. But the old country was no less safe from cheese crime and sin. Soon he found himself producing cheese for a local cheese mafia. And to make any money, the cheese Michael had to make wasn't exactly "clean". Cheese laced with mushrooms that broadened the senses, mold that could poison someone you wanted to get rid of, and a wine-washed crust strong enough to fail a priest, but subtle enough to pass a roadside sobriety test.

And today, he needs to make some cheese...
